### High-resolution focus textures and Earth PBR maps

The `highres/` directory contains the focus-view derivatives. The Sun, Mercury, Venus,
Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn's rings, Ceres, Eris, Haumea, and Makemake use the
4K–8K Solar System Scope files mirrored in the
[Wikimedia Commons Solar System Scope texture category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Solar_System_Scope_textures).
The same Solar System Scope / INOVE CC BY 4.0 attribution applies. JPEG sources are preserved
at their delivered resolution; WebP files are resized or losslessly encoded derivatives made
by `scripts/generate-surface-assets.mjs`.

Earth's `highres/earth_roughness.webp` is derived from the 8K Solar System Scope specular map
by inversion and level adjustment: ocean pixels remain smooth/dark while land and ice are
rough/light. The renderer also uses the 8K day, cloud, and night maps in focus view. These are
visual maps, not current weather products.

The 4K albedo derivatives for Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione,
Rhea, Titan, Iapetus, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Miranda, and Pluto upscale the mission-derived
maps documented below. Their corresponding `_height.webp` and `_normal.webp` files are
deterministic artistic relief generated by the repository script; they are not mission DEMs.

The Earth cloud and night maps were retrieved through their Wikimedia Commons mirrors;
their file pages identify Solar System Scope as the author and retain the same CC BY 4.0
license. The renderer uses the night map only on the hemisphere facing away from the simulated
Sun. The cloud layer is a visual atmosphere approximation, not a live weather dataset.

## Scientific DEM derivatives

The following focus-view height and tangent-space normal maps are downsampled visual
derivatives of public U.S. government elevation products. Heights are normalized per body for
rendering and therefore must not be used for measurement:

- `highres/moon_height.webp` and `highres/moon_normal.webp` — LOLA elevation from NASA
  Goddard's [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/); the matching 4K lunar color map is
  an LRO/LOLA derivative from the same kit. Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific
  Visualization Studio, LRO/LOLA science teams.
- `highres/earth_height.webp` and `highres/earth_normal.webp` — NOAA NCEI
  [ETOPO 2022 global relief model](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model),
  60 arc-second ice-surface elevation. Bathymetry is clamped to sea level so the renderer
  displaces the solid land surface without sinking the visible ocean; credit: NOAA National
  Centers for Environmental Information, DOI 10.25921/fd45-gt74.
- `highres/mars_height.webp` and `highres/mars_normal.webp` —
  [Mars MGS MOLA Global DEM 463 m](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_mgs_mola_dem_463m),
  USGS Astrogeology / NASA Mars Global Surveyor MOLA; public domain.
- `highres/mercury_height.webp` and `highres/mercury_normal.webp` —
  [Mercury MESSENGER Global DEM 665 m](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mercury_messenger_global_dem_665m),
  USGS Astrogeology / NASA MESSENGER; public domain.

`scripts/generate-surface-assets.mjs` records the repeatable normalization, filtering, and
normal-map conversion. The committed outputs are 4096 × 2048 so they remain practical for a
desktop GPU; the much larger scientific source GeoTIFFs are intentionally not committed.

## Clearly labelled artistic reconstructions

Complete global mission imagery does not exist for every listed body. Rather than stretching a
partial mosaic over an entire sphere or leaving black regions, the following 4K surfaces use
deterministic, repository-generated artistic albedo and relief based on broad observed color
families: Oberon, Triton, Charon, Vesta, Pallas, Hygiea, Psyche, Chiron, and Arrokoth. Ceres,
Eris, Haumea, and Makemake use the Solar System Scope fictional-completion albedo with
repository-generated artistic relief. These surfaces are marked “艺术重建” in the interface
and must not be interpreted as measured global maps.

## Uranus ring texture

`uranus_ring.png` is adapted without pixel changes from John van Vliet's
[Uranus (Artistic)](http://www.celestiamotherlode.net/addon/addon_1575.html) Celestia add-on,
licensed CC BY-SA. The author derived the radial ring positions from NASA/JPL Voyager 2
[PIA00142](https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA00142) imagery and slightly increased
brightness for visibility. Credit: John van Vliet, CC BY-SA; Voyager 2 source data: NASA/JPL.

## J2000 star background

`starfield-j2000-8k.jpg` is an sRGB JPEG conversion of the 8192 × 4096 OpenEXR map from
[NASA Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/).
It plots the positions, brightness, and colors of stars from Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2, Gaia DR2,
the Yale Bright Star Catalog, UCAC3, and XHIP in ICRF/J2000 celestial coordinates. Credit:
NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio; Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
The conversion applies display gamma and contrast only; star positions are unchanged.
